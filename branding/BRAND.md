# LithoCloud — logo and colour

**Mohammad Niknezhad · ÉTS Montréal** · github.com/MohammadNiknezhad/lithocloud

---

## The mark

A point cloud resolving into two fitted discontinuity planes, offset along dip: points
in, planes out. The two planes are the identical shape — one is the other moved down and
along. Everything is drawn on a 64-unit grid with a 2-unit outer margin, from disjoint
shapes only, which is why the one-colour versions are the same artwork rather than a
redraw.

ÉTS never appears inside the mark. It appears only as plain-text attribution:

> Mohammad Niknezhad · ÉTS Montréal

## Two cuts of the icon

| Cut | Used at | What differs |
|---|---|---|
| Full mark | 32 px and above | Twelve points on an even lattice |
| Small cut | 24, 20, 16 px | Four points on the dip line, wider gaps between planes |

Below ~32 px twelve points merge into a smear, so the small cut exists to keep the icon
readable in the taskbar. `lithocloud.ico` already contains both — Windows picks the right
frame automatically. Use `lithocloud-mark-small.svg` anywhere else you are drawing the
icon under 32 px yourself.

## Colour

| Role | Hex | Where |
|---|---|---|
| ÉTS red | `#DE002B` | Upper plane, "Cloud" in the wordmark |
| Red tint | `#FF4B66` | The points |
| Red shade | `#9C001E` | Lower plane |
| Ink | `#1B2430` | "Litho" in the wordmark |
| Tagline grey | `#5A6470` | "SEE INTO THE ROCK" |

`#DE002B` is sampled directly from the ÉTS logo. The tint and shade are a lightening and
a darkening of that one hue — there is no second colour in the system.

On dark backgrounds use `-mono-white`. For greyscale journal printing use `-mono-black`,
which prints as solid black rather than as three muddy greys.

## Typeface

The wordmark is **TeX Gyre Heros Bold** (a Helvetica metric clone, GUST licence),
converted to outlines. The SVGs carry no font dependency — nothing to install, identical
rendering in any viewer or PDF pipeline. For running text beside the logo, Arial,
Helvetica or Segoe UI all sit correctly with it.

## Clear space and minimum size

- **Clear space**: on every side, at least the height of one plane — about 28 % of the
  mark's height. Nothing else enters that band.
- **Minimum size, mark**: 16 px on screen (small cut), 6 mm in print.
- **Minimum size, lockup**: 120 px wide on screen, 30 mm in print. Below that, drop the
  tagline (`lithocloud-lockup-no-tagline.svg`); below 90 px, use the mark alone.

## Don't

- Recolour outside the five values above, or apply gradients, shadows or outlines.
- Stretch, rotate, or change the spacing between the cloud and the planes.
- Place the colour mark on a red background — use `-mono-white` instead.
- Put ÉTS, a supervisor's name, or any other lettering inside the mark.
- Rebuild the wordmark from a system font; use the outlined SVG.

svg/

png/

  lithocloud-mark-small-256.png

`make_mark.py` is dependency-free and reproduces the mark artwork exactly. The wordmark
is not regenerated by it — it is already outlined in the SVGs.

## Where to use what

- **Windows app / taskbar / installer** — `ico/lithocloud.ico`
- **GitHub repo social preview and README** — `png/lithocloud-lockup-800.png`
- **GitHub avatar** — `png/lithocloud-mark-512.png` (it survives the circular crop)
- **Slides** — `png/lithocloud-lockup-1600.png`, or the mono-white version on dark slides
- **Papers and theses** — `svg/lithocloud-lockup-mono-black.svg` at 30 mm or wider
